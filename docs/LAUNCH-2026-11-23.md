## Release handoff

After the gates pass, Christian merges the `staging` → `main` release PR and
watches the Workers production deployment. Then, in a private window, he checks
the public home page and `/play`, confirms both `/signin` and `/signup` still
say accounts open soon, and confirms “get launch updates” opens
`https://cueory.kit.com/bfd9609b46` in a new tab with no inline signup form.
He also rechecks rendered `/terms` and `/disclaimer` against approved Terms v6,
verifies `/privacy` separately, and confirms the staging preview remains behind
Cloudflare Access.

Before Christian merges, a failed check means hold the release candidate: do not
merge it, and leave the current production deployment in place. After a release
has deployed, a failed production check means roll back the deployed release to
the last known-good production deployment, then send the failure details to the
lead.
