  help, result cards and device-local statistics; phone, landscape and large-text
  layout repairs. Game-specific routes were added in #816. At this snapshot,

  been updated, and storage keys are centrally tracked. These edits do not supply

  budgets, content validation and release checks. Documentation and tests also
  record unfinished setup and founder-held work.

can affect user-facing behavior; `no` means documentation/tooling/build/dependency/checks only.

database migration or deployment is active. The Games, Accounts, Legal and Infra labels are

entry; the group does not identify its main purpose or release readiness.
