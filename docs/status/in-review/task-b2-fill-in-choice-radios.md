The CSP is unchanged.
- Change: the four choices are a labelled radio group ("the missing word"). Each is a 44 px row with a 24 px radio. Arrow keys move between them, Space picks one, Enter checks the chosen answer, and a tap on the row picks it. "type instead" and "choose from options" work as before, and a typed answer that isn't one of the choices still can't be checked after switching back. Focus goes to the chosen answer, or to the first one when none is chosen.
